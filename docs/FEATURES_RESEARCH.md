# Feature & Market Research — WanderPG

Research date: October 2026.

## 1. Competitive Landscape (India PG Market)
- Existing platforms include NoBroker, RentOk, Stayz, InstaDwell.
- **Key Differentiator**: Most competitors are city-centric; WanderPG is **college-first**. A student selects their university/college and immediately sees verified PGs ranked by real walking distance.

## 2. Feature Gaps Students Experience
- Verified owners and photos (eradicating fake or misleading property images).
- Clear total monthly cost (rent, electricity, security deposit, maintenance, food options).
- Safety indicators: CCTV, gender-specific housing, gate closing timings.
- Proximity to campus, transport links, tiffin services.
- Direct contact without broker middlemen or spam calling.

## 3. Mapbox Geocoding Storage Rule
- Default Mapbox geocoding is temporary: storing coordinates without permissions violates Mapbox Terms of Service.
- Forward geocoding requests where coordinates are persisted require `permanent=true` parameter.
- Confirm pricing tier for permanent geocoding before enabling on live account.

## 4. India Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)
- Notice and explicit consent required for personal data (name, email, phone number).
- Data minimization and purpose limitation.
- Account deletion and data removal requests honoured within 30 days.
- Privacy notice and consent checkbox required on signup.
